About

About the Owner

Muhammad Ibrar is the founder and owner of Preparation for All Types of Tests of ETEA KPK. He started this platform after seeing how many candidates in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa struggle to find well-organised, free and reliable practice material for job tests.

His aim is simple: to give every candidate, from any city or village, the same quality of preparation, and to keep improving the platform by adding new questions regularly.

About this Website

This is an online preparation platform built for ETEA KPK test candidates. It gives you two things for every subject:

  • Notes: subject-wise study notes for every post.
  • MCQs: four options, one correct answer and a short explanation, so you understand the concept and do not just memorise.

The website is updated regularly and everything is stored online, so nothing is lost. Want to help? You can also add your own MCQs and notes, and other candidates will benefit from them.

Our Purpose

  • Make ETEA test preparation easy, free and accessible to everyone.
  • Cover all major posts and subjects in one place, with notes and MCQs.
  • Build confidence through regular practice and clear explanations.
  • Let the community contribute new MCQs and notes to keep content fresh.

Who is it for?

Anyone preparing for ETEA tests: Project Management, Project Coordinator, MIS, Accountant, Computer Operator, Junior Clerk, Office Assistant, Monitoring & Evaluation, and the common subjects English, Statistics and Research Methodology.
